A make.fun entry for "Two-Sentence Horror" — one horror story of exactly two
sentences, up to 400 characters, judged pairwise by a house judge.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My son's school photo came home yesterday, and in the back row stands a boy in my son's clothes, with my son's face, smiling at the camera. My son says the boy is new, that he sits beside him every day, and that yesterday he asked if he could borrow his name.

Run it with `node main.ts` (Node 22+, no dependencies). The output is 259
characters of plain UTF-8 text with no trailing newline.
